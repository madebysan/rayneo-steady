RayNeo Steady 0.2.1
For Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 15 or later.

INSTALL
Drag RayNeo Steady into Applications, then open it.
The eyeglasses icon in the menu bar opens the controls.

SHOW YOUR DESKTOP
1. Connect your RayNeo Air 4 Pro and close other glasses apps.
2. Choose “My Mac desktop” under Show.
3. Choose your Mac screen under Source and SmartGlasses under Output.
4. Click Start in Glasses. Allow Screen Recording when macOS asks.
5. If macOS requires it, quit and reopen RayNeo Steady after granting access.

The desktop replaces the size-guide grid and uses the same size and tracking
controls. Your desktop frames stay on this Mac; the app does not upload or save
screen recordings. Keep your pointer on the source screen to interact normally.

For desktop output in the glasses, macOS must expose the source screen and
SmartGlasses as separate displays. RayNeo Steady does not change your macOS
display arrangement. The calibration grid and windowed preview also work in
a mirrored setup.

COMFORT
Start at 80% screen size. Use − and + for exact one-point adjustments.
Rest the glasses on a still surface until calibration is ready, then put them
on and Recenter. Pinned holds a direction; Steady Follow gradually follows
deliberate head turns. Refined tracking can be toggled live for comparison.
Calibration grid is an optional size guide, not the normal desktop view.

SHORTCUTS
Control–Command–1: show controls
Control–Command–R: recenter
Control–Command–S: stop output

NOTES
Head tracking is experimental. Some micro-shakes and long-term drift may remain.
This is rotational tracking, not room-position tracking. Protected video may
not be capturable. Closing the controls leaves the menu-bar app running.
